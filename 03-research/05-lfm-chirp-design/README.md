# 05-lfm-chirp-design

## What this folder supports

Why a linear frequency modulated sweep, and how the sweep is parameterised.

## Where it is used in our work

`synthesise()` mode 'lfm'; the fStart/fStop selection in `decide()`; the Waveform screen.

## Note

Radar-dominant literature. The mathematics of LFM waveform design transfers directly to sonar; what changes is the propagation medium and the timescale.
