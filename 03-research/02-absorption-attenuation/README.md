# 02-absorption-attenuation

## What this folder supports

Frequency-dependent absorption in sea water: the term that makes high frequency expensive over range.

## Where it is used in our work

`thorpDbPerKm()` and `absorptionDbPerM()`; the loss curve in Figure 1 of the notebook; the range-versus-resolution argument.

## Note

We implement Thorp's expression. These papers establish the mechanism, the measured behaviour of propagation loss, and the conditions under which simplified models hold.
