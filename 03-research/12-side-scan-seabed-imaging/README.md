# 12-side-scan-seabed-imaging

## What this folder supports

The application: what a side-scan payload is ultimately for, and what image quality depends on.

## Where it is used in our work

The PPI display and contact list; the resolution argument; the problem framing in the report.

## Note

Establishes the survey context, why range resolution matters for imaging, and how seabed returns are classified.
