# 03-sediment-scattering

## What this folder supports

Excess attenuation from suspended sediment, which rises steeply with frequency and is what forces the payload downband.

## Where it is used in our work

`scatterDbPerM()` and the `scatterCoeff` / `scatterRefHz` constants; the turbidity axis of the adaptation.

## Note

Our scattering term is an engineering model referenced to the medium's band. These papers establish that acoustic attenuation and backscatter track suspended sediment concentration, which is the physical basis for treating turbidity as a control input.
