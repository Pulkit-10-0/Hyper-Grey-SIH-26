# 01-sound-speed

## What this folder supports

Sound speed in water, and why a range estimate is only as good as the sound speed used to compute it.

## Where it is used in our work

`src/core/physics.ts` soundSpeedWater() and soundSpeedAir(); the Env screen; the notebook Section 1.

## Note

Mackenzie's nine-term equation is the one we implement. These papers establish the profile problem, how sound speed is measured and estimated in the field, and how sensitive acoustic ranging is to getting it wrong.
