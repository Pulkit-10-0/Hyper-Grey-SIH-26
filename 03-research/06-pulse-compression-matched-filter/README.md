# 06-pulse-compression-matched-filter

## What this folder supports

Matched filtering, compression gain, and range resolution decoupled from pulse energy.

## Where it is used in our work

`matchedFilter()`, `compressionGainDb()`, `rangeResolutionChirp()`; the Echo screen; notebook Section 5.

## Note

The central technique of the whole project: a 5 ms pulse resolving 3.75 m unmodulated resolves 7.5 mm compressed, at identical transmitted energy.
