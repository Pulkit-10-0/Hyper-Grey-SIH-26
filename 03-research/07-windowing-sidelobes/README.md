# 07-windowing-sidelobes

## What this folder supports

The transmit envelope window, its sidelobe floor, and why the pulse edges are tapered.

## Where it is used in our work

`windowFunction()` and `WINDOW_PSL_DB`; the window selector on the Waveform screen; the PS clause on protecting the output stage.

## Note

Establishes the sidelobe levels we quote (rectangular -13.3 dB through Blackman -58 dB) and the sidelobe-suppression techniques beyond simple weighting.
