# 13-auv-platforms-survey

## What this folder supports

The host platform, its mission profile, and the constraints a payload inherits from it.

## Where it is used in our work

The Mission screen; the endurance model; the form-factor requirement in `01-hardware/enclosure`.

## Note

Establishes that the vehicle's energy budget and mission duration are the binding constraints on any payload it carries.
