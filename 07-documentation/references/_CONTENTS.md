# references

## Purpose
The literature the design rests on, held locally so nothing depends on a link.

## Files that must exist
- `references.bib`
- `references.md` — numbered list matching the report
- `papers/` — the PDFs, filenames matching the citation key
- `reading-notes.md` — one paragraph per source: what was used from it

## The core set
- Ainslie and McColm, simplified absorption formula, JASA 1998
- Francois and Garrison, sound absorption from ocean measurements, JASA 1982
- Mackenzie, nine-term sound speed equation, JASA 1981
- A recent cognitive or adaptive sonar waveform design paper, 2023 or later
- A wideband sonar waveform design paper, 2023 or later
- National Physical Laboratory, absorption of sound in seawater
- Ministry of Earth Sciences or NIOT primary source on the Deep Ocean Mission

## Acceptance
Every citation in the report resolves to a PDF in `papers/`. `reading-notes.md`
proves the sources were read rather than collected: each entry says what
equation or figure was actually used and where it appears in the design.

## What went wrong last year
Fifteen reference PDFs were present with no notes, so there was no way to tell
which ones informed the design and which were decoration.
