\subsection{Why only nine centres are ever selected}

Bandwidth is clipped so the sweep stays inside the transducer band:
$B \leq 2\min(f_c - f_{\text{lo}},\, f_{\text{hi}} - f_c)$ and separately
$B \leq 0.9 f_c$. Together those make achievable bandwidth peak at
\SI{350.7}{\kilo\hertz} and fall away on either side.

\section{Selection rule, stated precisely}
% =====================================================================

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
\text{margin}(N) &= 3 + \min(12,\; N/70) \quad \si{\decibel} \\
\text{NL}(N) &= 52 + \min(8,\; N/140) \quad \si{\decibel} \\
\text{feasible} &= \bigl(\mathrm{SNR} \geq \mathrm{DT} + \text{margin}\bigr)
   \;\wedge\; \bigl(E \leq E_{\max}\bigr)
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}

If no candidate is feasible the constraint relaxes to
$\mathrm{SNR} \geq \mathrm{DT}$. If that set is also empty the candidate with
the greatest predicted SNR is selected and the decision is flagged infeasible,
so the mission is told the request cannot be met rather than being handed a
pulse that appears ordinary. Ties are broken on widest bandwidth first, then
lowest energy.

Mode and window follow from the achieved margin $m = \mathrm{SNR} - \mathrm{DT}$:

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Condition} & \textbf{Mode} & \textbf{Window} \\
\midrule
$m < \SI{2}{\decibel}$ & Barker-13 & Hann \\
$m < \SI{3}{\decibel}$ & LFM or geometric & Hann \\
Depth $> \SI{90}{\metre}$ & Geometric & by turbidity \\
$N > \SI{400}{\NTU}$ & by margin & Blackman \\
Otherwise & LFM & Hamming \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Mode and window selection.}
\end{table}
