# user-guide

## Purpose
How to operate the thing, for somebody who did not build it.

## Files that must exist
- `user-guide.pdf` — **8 to 10 pages**, screenshots throughout
- `quick-start.pdf` — **1 page**, laminate it for the table
- `screenshots/`
- `troubleshooting.md`

## Sections
Powering on, the two modes and when to use each, reading each screen, running a
ping, interpreting the echo, exporting a session, what each fault means, and
what to do when the link drops.

## Facts and figures this must carry
- One annotated screenshot per screen
- A table of every status value and what it means
- The units of every displayed quantity
- Battery life at the configured ping rate

## Acceptance
Hand it to somebody outside the team with the phone and the pod. They should
run a full ping cycle without asking a question.

## What went wrong last year
Two duplicate readme files, no screenshots, no content.
