THE PROBLEM, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
A conventional fixed ping cannot be ideal for every water condition or mission.
Propagation, scattering, source efficiency, resolution and energy use change
with frequency, waveform bandwidth, pulse length and drive level. PS 26058
therefore calls for physical embedded hardware that reads sensors or analog
controls, selects sonar waveform parameters in real time, and produces an analog
output through a DAC and amplifier while conserving AUV energy. A phone UI,
synthetic FFT or waveform plot by itself does not satisfy that physical demand.

THE CORE SYSTEM IDEA
Environmental acquisition -> bounded decision logic -> waveform generation
-> timed transfer -> DAC -> reconstruction/filtering -> output stage ->
transducer. A local operator console presents settings and trace data, while
a test dossier ties every acceptance claim to a specific recording.

A team constraint is that a fully functional deployed hardware build is not
part of this submission exercise. The presentation can still show the MVP,
source, board design, interface plan, verification method and product pathway.
Do not convert that constraint into a claim that an unbuilt unit passed tests.

THREE CONFIGURATIONS. NEVER COMBINE THEIR NUMBERS.
1. Bench/RevB configuration:
   ESP32-S3 external development board, DS18B20, ADS1115 sensor modules,
   potentiometers, 40 kHz LEDC PWM transmitter path, TC4426 gate driver,
   IRLZ44N MOSFET, GU1008C-40T nominal 40 kHz TX element, a separate classic
   ESP32 receive section and separate I2S/MAX98357A 1 kHz audio output. The
   KiCad RevB PCB is a 100 x 100 mm, two-layer, 91-reference design. Its
   Gerbers and assembly information live in 01-hardware. It has no parallel
   sonar DAC. The older serial session in 02-firmware/measurements/captures
   shows sensor and pot values changing, not adaptive physical sonar output.
2. ESP-IDF payload source:
   ESP32-S3 / FreeRTOS, external ADS1115 sensor reads, rule-based solver,
   fixed-point sine-LUT DDS, windowing, DMA-capable I80 transfer through two
   1024-byte buffers, INA226 power-monitor source and BLE telemetry. Candidate
   tones are 24, 32, 40, 48, 60 and 80 kHz. Nominal software sample-rate
   constant is 2 MSps; the I80 pixel clock is configured at 20 MHz. A matching
   DAC/analog path and an instrument trace are needed to establish actual
   effective sample cadence and analog output.
3. Product/model study:
   A generated 20,000-row, 27-column dataset and 468-candidate laptop-side
   selection method study an assumed 100-500 kHz transducer band. Winning
   center frequencies in the published synthetic data are 148-351 kHz.
   These are not measured operating frequencies of the RevB 40 kHz circuit.

TECHNICAL DIFFERENTIATION
- Decision logic is bounded by acoustic feasibility, electrical limits and
  an energy budget rather than choosing the visually prettiest waveform.
- The intended waveforms are LFM chirps, geometric/exponential sweeps and
  phase-coded pulses. A constant-frequency sine with a tapered envelope is a
  useful DDS primitive but not any of these modulations.
- Windowed pulse edges reduce switching abruptness and can reduce spectral
  leakage; Hann, Tukey and Blackman are present in the inspected ESP-IDF source.
- A DMA-capable peripheral is used for block transfer. Continuous,
  timer-paced, gap-free sample streaming must be established with a trace.
- The phone is an operator layer; acoustic transmission must remain an
  embedded hardware responsibility.
- The India case includes a narrow side-scan-sonar TAM and bottom-up SAM/SOM
  analysis in 09-business, rather than treating all marine spending as demand.

ACOUSTIC REASONING FOR SPEAKERS
Higher usable bandwidth improves compressed-pulse range resolution, roughly
c/(2B) in the ideal case. Higher frequency also generally increases seawater
absorption, while acoustic loss from suspended sediment depends on concentration,
particle-size distribution and frequency. Temperature, salinity and depth
affect propagation. Turbidity in NTU is an optical proxy, not an absolute
acoustic attenuation measurement. A slide can explain the tradeoff, but it
must not claim a universal numerical NTU-to-dB rule without calibration.
Source pointers: 03-research, 07-documentation/references, and the external
acoustics links in PS26058-technical-audit.md.

PRESENTER Q&A ANCHORS
Q: Is a mobile waveform plot a sonar transmitter?
A: No. The physical output path is the MCU through DAC, conditioning and
driver; the app is an operator and analysis layer.
Q: Is a potentiometer acceptable as an environment input?
A: Yes under PS 26058, provided its ADC-to-waveform path is demonstrated.
Q: Are the 40 kHz demonstrator and the 100-500 kHz study one device?
A: No. They are different configurations with different transducers.
Q: Does DMA alone prove low power?
A: No. Energy needs a time-aligned voltage/current integral per ping.
Q: Can the repo itself prove underwater readiness?
A: No. It can provide source, manufacturing files, procedures and raw records;
waterproofing, acoustic source level and vehicle operation need physical
qualification.
