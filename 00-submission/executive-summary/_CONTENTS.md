# executive-summary

## Purpose
What a judge reads if they read exactly one thing. Written last, after every
number is final.

## Files that must exist
- `executive-summary.pdf` — **2 pages, hard limit**
- `one-pager.pdf` — **1 page**, for the table

## Structure of the 2-page version
1. The problem, in three sentences, with the physical reason it is hard
2. What we built, one paragraph, no adjectives
3. The architecture diagram, half a page
4. Results table: six to eight measured numbers
5. What is real today and what is not, stated plainly
6. Path to deployment: three bullets naming the actual next parts

## Facts and figures this must carry
- The frequency swing under changing turbidity, with both endpoints
- Resolution improvement from pulse compression, as a ratio
- Energy per ping and endurance
- Test counts: physics assertions, render tests, screens
- The one limitation you volunteer before being asked

## Acceptance
No sentence longer than 25 words. No claim without a number behind it. If a
figure has no source in `05-validation`, delete the sentence.

## What went wrong last year
The README was excellent but 12 KB long. There was no two-page door into it.
