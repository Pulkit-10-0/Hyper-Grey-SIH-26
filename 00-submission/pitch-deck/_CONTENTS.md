# pitch-deck

## Purpose
The deck shown at the table. Built to be talked over, not read.

## Files that must exist
- `pitch-deck.pdf` — **12 to 15 slides**, exported flat
- `pitch-deck.pptx` or `.key` — the editable source
- `speaker-notes.md` — timed, per slide
- `backup-slides.pdf` — the ten answers to the ten questions you expect

## Slide order that works
1. Title, team, problem statement number
2. The problem, with the physical mechanism
3. Why a fixed ping is the wrong instrument
4. What we built, one architecture diagram
5. The adaptation, with the before and after numbers
6. Pulse compression, with the resolution ratio
7. Hardware, with a photograph of the actual board
8. Power, with the measured table
9. Validation, with the test counts
10. The learned model and its measured failure rate
11. What is not real yet
12. Path to deployment, with real part numbers
13. Team and timeline

## Facts and figures this must carry
Every slide with a claim carries the number and the units. Slide 11 is not
optional and is the slide that separates you.

## Acceptance
No slide has more than 25 words. No slide has a bullet list longer than four
items. Every chart has axis labels with units.
