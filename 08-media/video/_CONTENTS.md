# video

## Purpose
Insurance and reach. If the hardware dies on the day, the video is the proof it
ever worked.

## Files that must exist
- `demo-full.mp4` — **4 to 6 minutes**, the complete run
- `demo-90s.mp4` — the cut for the judging table
- `dunk-test.mp4` — **30 to 45 seconds**, sealed pod, opened dry
- `adaptation-clip.mp4` — sediment added, frequency drops, unaided
- `youtube-links.md`
- `captions/` — SRT files

## What the full video must show
Bench in one continuous shot where possible. A judge changing the water. The
scope trace beside the phone. The idle-hook capture. The pod opened after
immersion.

## Rules
- Shot in landscape, stable, audible
- Every on-screen number legible at 720p
- No music over spoken explanation
- Anything simulated is said out loud at the moment it appears

## Acceptance
Somebody who never sees the hardware understands what it does and believes it
works. Uploaded and linked before the finale, not the night of.
