# photos

## Purpose
The submission is judged partly on whether it looks like engineering.

## Files that must exist
- `hardware/` — board top and bottom, populated, in focus, plain background
- `bench/` — the full rig, labelled
- `tank/` — the water setup with transducers and target
- `enclosure/` — pod open, closed, and in the hull section
- `team/` — one usable group photograph
- `captions.md` — one line per photograph

## Standards
- Plain background, even light, no flash glare, no phone shadow
- The board photographed square-on, high enough resolution to read the silkscreen
- A scale reference in mechanical shots
- Cables dressed before the shot

## Facts each caption must carry
What it shows, board revision or build date, and where the same item appears in
the documentation.

## Acceptance
The board photograph is good enough to print in the report at half a page and
still read the revision on the silkscreen.
