# team

## Purpose
Who did what, and who can answer for it under questioning.

## Files that must exist
- `team.md` — name, role, contribution, contact
- `ownership.md` — every subsystem with a primary and a backup owner
- `qa-readiness.md` — who answers which category of question

## Ownership table
Every subsystem needs two names. If only one person understands the firmware
and they are ill on the day, the submission loses that subsystem.

Subsystems: physics and solver, firmware, analog hardware, PCB, mechanical,
mobile app, notebook and model, documentation, demo operation.

## Question categories to assign
Acoustics and physics, embedded and real-time, analog design, power, software
architecture, the machine learning component, market and deployment.

## Acceptance
No subsystem has a single point of failure. Each owner can explain their area
for two minutes without slides.
