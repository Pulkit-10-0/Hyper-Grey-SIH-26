## Hardware and external material

Manufacturer datasheets, product photographs, logos and diagrams remain owned by their publishers. Link to them as sources rather than embedding them in distributable project assets unless permission or an applicable licence is recorded. Team-generated drawings, graphs and photographs should carry author, capture date and project copyright metadata.

## Release records

The release owner will generate a transitive dependency list from the final lockfiles, attach the corresponding licence texts, identify copied code or adapted algorithms, and save the notice bundle with the release tag. Any dependency with unknown, copyleft, non-commercial or field-of-use terms is a stop condition until reviewed.
