# Disclosure and filing control

## Filing objective

File an Indian provisional patent application by 25 September 2026 for the physics-constrained embedded adaptive acoustic controller. Keep the learned-surrogate claim set in the same first filing only if the search confirms unity and adequate technical detail; otherwise preserve it as a second family.

## Public-disclosure rule

Before the filing receipt is archived, do not publish enabling implementation details in a public repository, paper, video, poster, competition upload or unrestricted presentation. External reviews must use access-controlled files and a disclosure record. After filing, public material may describe the system within the filed support and must avoid adding an unfiled improvement as if it were already protected.

## Filing pack

- Form 1 applicant/inventor details and assignment evidence where required.
- Form 2 provisional specification with architecture drawings and alternatives.
- Form 3 statement and undertaking.
- Form 5 inventorship declaration when applicable.
- Form 28 startup/small-entity evidence when the applicant qualifies.
- Source archive, lab figures, dated engineering notes and contributor record.

## Improvement log

Every improvement after the filing gate receives an internal disclosure number, date, contributors, technical difference, evidence and public-disclosure status. Patent counsel decides whether it belongs in the complete specification or a separate filing.
