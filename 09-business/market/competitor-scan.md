# Competitor and alternative scan

## Positioning

SeaNergy is positioned as an Indian, low-cost adaptive acoustic payload and development platform. It should not be presented as a direct replacement for every side-scan, scanning or multibeam sonar. The commercial wedge is rapid environmental adaptation, transparent decision traces, low per-ping energy, local integration and a price suitable for research, inspection and autonomous-platform pilots.

## Defensible differentiators

1. Physics-constrained waveform selection runs on the embedded controller without cloud access.
2. A learned surrogate can accelerate selection but cannot bypass electrical, acoustic or timing guards.
3. Every ping exposes waveform parameters, estimated margin, energy and decision reason.
4. The transmit-side dataset and replay path make the decision process auditable.
5. India-based support, INR procurement and locally replaceable subsystems reduce pilot friction.

## Procurement comparison

The target pilot selling price is INR 1.95 lakh ex-GST with COGS of about INR 1.18 lakh and gross margin near 39.7%. Imported benchmarks must add freight, customs, GST, currency movement, mounting, integration and support. Comparisons should be described as procurement benchmarks, not feature equivalence.

## Go-to-market sequence

Start with paid evaluation kits for universities, AUV/ROV teams and inland-water inspection groups. Convert successful evaluations into port, defence-innovation and ocean-research pilots. Add calibrated receive chains, rugged pressure-rated packaging and survey-software integration before competing for higher-value operational payloads.
