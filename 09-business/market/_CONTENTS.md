# market

## Purpose
The commercial case, sized in rupees with sources.

## Files that must exist
- `market-sizing.pdf` — **3 to 4 pages**
- `sources.md` — every figure with its citation and access date
- `competitor-scan.md`
- `cost-model.xlsx`

## What must be quantified
| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| TAM | Global side-scan sonar and acoustic payload spend, INR, with the USD source and the exchange rate used |
| SAM | Indian serviceable segment, built up from named programmes |
| SOM | Obtainable in five years, staged year by year, with the constraint named |
| Unit economics | BOM cost, assembly, margin, price point |
| Comparison | Imported system price against ours |

## Rules
- State the exchange rate used and the date
- Every market figure cites a named report, not a blog aggregating one
- The SAM is built bottom-up from real programmes and budgets, not a percentage
  of TAM pulled from the air
- The constraint on SOM is named: qualification and sea trials, not demand

## Acceptance
A judge can check any figure against its source in under a minute. Nothing is
rounded upward to look better.
