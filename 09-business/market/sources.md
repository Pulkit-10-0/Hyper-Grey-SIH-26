## Method notes

The TAM uses a narrow side-scan sonar category rather than the much larger ocean-technology market. USD values are converted using the latest available RBI reference-rate archive value before preparation. The Indian SAM is bottom-up: estimated deployable units multiplied by target selling price across named customer groups. It is not calculated as an arbitrary percentage of government programme budgets.

All supplier prices exclude freight, customs duty, GST, integration and local support unless stated. Planning estimates are deliberately labeled and should be replaced by written quotations during procurement.
