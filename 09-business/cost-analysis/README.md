# India cost and market analysis

The [saved PDF](SeaNergy_India_Cost_Market_Analysis.pdf) and [LaTeX source](SeaNergy_India_Cost_Market_Analysis.tex) explain the project's cost structure, pricing scenarios, TAM/SAM/SOM and sensitivity assumptions. [make_figures.py](make_figures.py) produces the charts in [figures](figures/README.md); `.aux` and `.out` files are saved compiler outputs.

The paper separates the bench demonstrator, evaluation unit, integrated programme and field-development reserve. Its INR values and fixed FX assumptions are planning estimates; the kit margin must not be applied to the different integrated-payload revenue scenario.

Use the editable [market workbook](../market/cost-model.xlsx) and [source register](../market/sources.md) when revising assumptions. Return to [business planning](../README.md).
