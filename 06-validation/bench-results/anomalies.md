# Deviation controls and final disposition

| Test | Engineering deviation | Control applied | Final result |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 | Resonance can shift with mounting and water loading | Solver searches the measured resonance neighbourhood | 40.2 kHz operating centre |
| T-02 | Driver dead time raises the third harmonic | Symmetric drive and reconstruction filter | 0.31% THD |
| T-04 | Window coefficient quantisation shifts sidelobes | Q15 coefficients and peak normalisation | Maximum theory error 0.51 dB |
| T-08 | Fixed sound speed creates temperature-dependent range bias | Temperature-compensated sound speed per run | 0.8 cm MAE |
| T-14 | Sediment settling changes path turbidity | Circulation followed by fixed settling interval | Monotonic frequency selection |
| T-16 | Surface water can bias post-immersion mass | Standardised wipe and 60 s drain interval | 0.1 g mass shift |
