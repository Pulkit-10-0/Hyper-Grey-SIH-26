# Instruments index

- `equipment-list.md`: asset IDs, capabilities, custodians and control status.
- `uncertainty.md`: expanded k=2 uncertainty budget.
- `limitations.md`: measurement boundaries and result applicability.
- `settings/test-settings.csv`: test-by-test configuration and data mapping.
- `settings/`: scope, FFT, tank and power acquisition procedures.

The instrument configuration supports 36 Hz expanded frequency uncertainty at
40 kHz, 0.60 dB spectral uncertainty and 4.2% complete-ping energy uncertainty.
