# Measurement boundaries

- Acoustic amplitude is expressed as relative dB re the fixed reference run.
- Tank walls are gated out with a range window beginning 80 mm after the target peak.
- The 450 L tank supports controlled near-field and medium-range scaling; open-water performance is extrapolated through the validated sonar equation.
- Turbidity runs use a circulation and settling protocol so the sensor and acoustic path share the same water condition.
- Range accuracy combines tape geometry, temperature-derived sound speed and matched-filter interpolation.
- Results apply to the documented Rev-A electronics, V2 enclosure, 2 MSps firmware and 24-80 kHz waveform set.
