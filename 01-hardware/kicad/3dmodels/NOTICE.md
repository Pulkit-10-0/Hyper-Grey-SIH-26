# Included KiCad 3D models

These models are copied unchanged from the official KiCad 9.0.7 installed
library, including their author and license headers. See
`LICENSE_KiCad_Libraries.md`, `../libraries/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt` and
`../libraries/NOTICE.md` for attribution, the license and the KiCad exception.
Older capacitor and DIP models retain GPL-3.0-or-later headers and their design
exception; `../libraries/GPL-3.0-or-later.txt` supplies the GNU license text.
See `LICENSES.md` for the per-model notice index. Individual original notices
are retained inside each model and take precedence over a collection summary.
STEP and corresponding VRML files are included when available. They retain
their original `<Library>.3dshapes/<Model>.<extension>` folder structure.
