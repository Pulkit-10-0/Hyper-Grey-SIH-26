# Enclosure seal design

Document ID: HW-MECH-003  
Basis: Parker O-Ring Handbook ORD 5700, static face-seal guidance, and AS568-246 standard size.

## 1. Selected prototype seal

| Parameter | Value | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| O-ring | AS568-246, NBR 70 Shore A | Selected target |
| Free inside diameter | 113.89 +/-0.76 mm | Standard dimension |
| Cross-section | 3.53 +/-0.10 mm | Standard dimension |
| Calculated outside diameter | 120.95 mm | ID + 2 x CS |
| Groove depth | 2.82 +0.00/-0.05 mm | Design target |
| Nominal axial squeeze | (3.53-2.82)/3.53 = 20.1% | Calculated |
| Groove width | 4.80 +0.10/-0.00 mm | Design target |
| Nominal gland fill | pi x 3.53^2 / 4 / (4.80 x 2.82) = 72.3% | Calculated |
| Groove OD | 121.00 mm | External pressure seats ring against outer wall |
| Seal-land finish | Ra <=0.8 um target | Requires machining/coating and inspection |

## 2. Material and media

NBR 70 is the freshwater prototype baseline. Confirm compatibility for seawater, lubricant, temperature and storage. FKM 75 is the listed substitute but is not automatically better at low temperature. Record compound and lot.

## 3. Assembly

Clean with lint-free material, inspect under bright light, apply a thin compatible lubricant film, place without twist, close squarely and torque twelve M3 fasteners in two cross-pattern passes to 0.50 N m.

## 4. Design holds

- Groove calculations must be tolerance-stacked at minimum and maximum O-ring cross-section.
- Printed surfaces cannot be assumed to meet finish or porosity requirements.
- Penetrator sealing geometry and manufacturer torque must be added after exact part selection.
- External-pressure finite-element review and shielded proof testing are required before any depth rating.
