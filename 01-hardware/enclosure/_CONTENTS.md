# enclosure

## Purpose
The payload pod, and the evidence it is a pressure housing rather than a box.

## Files that must exist
- `cad/` — source CAD, STEP export, and STL for printing
- `drawings.pdf` — **2 to 3 pages**, orthographic with dimensions and tolerances
- `print-settings.md` — material, layer height, infill, wall count, orientation
- `seal-design.md` — o-ring groove dimensions, compression, material
- `dunk-test.md` — procedure and result
- `hull-section/` — the mating AUV hull segment used to show fit

## Facts and figures this must carry
- External dimensions, internal volume, mass in air, estimated mass in water
- O-ring size, groove width and depth, calculated compression percentage
- Penetrator or cable gland part and its pressure rating
- Print material, and why (PETG against PLA against resin)
- Dunk test: depth, duration, result, with photographs of dry tissue inside

## Acceptance
The drawings carry real dimensions and tolerances. The o-ring groove is
dimensioned from a standard, not eyeballed. The dunk test has a video.

There was no mechanical folder at all.
