# Hull-section fit envelope

- [fit-envelope.md](fit-envelope.md) records the 168 × 210 × 40 mm payload envelope, service access and clearance assumptions.
- [hull-section-outline.svg](hull-section-outline.svg) is the printable fit outline. Print at 100% and verify its 100 mm reference bar before use.

The render/STL envelope and scaled outline support installation planning and physical fit checks. Return to the [enclosure guide](../README.md) for CAD files and sealing requirements.
