## 2. Procedure

1. Fit dry blue indicator tissue under the lowest penetrator and around the access-cap perimeter.
2. Photograph tissue, O-ring, seal land, penetrators and torque record.
3. Weigh the closed unit, record water temperature and immerse without energising electronics.
4. Observe continuously during Stage 1. After each stage, dry the exterior before opening.
5. Photograph tissue before touching it and weigh the unit again.
6. A leak, crack, permanent deformation or >0.5 g mass increase is a failure.

## 3. Safety

Hydrostatic pressure stores energy. Use a certified shielded vessel and remote observation. Never pressurise the V1 print or an occupied/hand-held improvised vessel.
