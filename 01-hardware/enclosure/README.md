# Enclosure and mechanical integration

The enclosure package includes the supplied V1 physical-form mesh, a V2 parametric sealed-housing concept, rendered assembly views, fit envelopes and fabrication guidance.

- [CAD files](cad/README.md): V1 STL mesh and V2 parametric OpenSCAD model.
- [Renders](renders/README.md): exterior, mesh and exploded design views.
- [Hull-section fit envelope](hull-section/README.md): installation clearances and printable outline.
- [Drawings](drawings.pdf): mechanical reference.
- [Print settings](print-settings.md): fabrication guidance.
- [Seal design](seal-design.md): O-ring geometry and mechanical design criteria.
- [Dunk and pressure test](dunk-test.md): acceptance-test procedure.

V1 provides the form and installation envelope; V2 defines the sealed-housing concept. Use the seal and pressure-test procedures when assessing a fabricated enclosure for its operating conditions. Allow space around the 100 × 100 mm compact PCB for external modules, wire bends and mounting support.
